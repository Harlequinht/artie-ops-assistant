artie-ops-assistant

Last updated: 5 September 2026.

artie-ops-assistant is a single-user, self-hosted personal assistant. It runs on one machine, under the control of one individual, against that individual's own Google account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

  • Monitors a single Gmail inbox and assigns each new message to a category.
  • Sends a private notification when a message appears to need attention.
  • Answers a summary command listing the day's mail by category.

What it does not do

  • It does not send, modify, delete, or archive email. The Gmail scope granted is read-only.
  • It does not serve any user other than its operator.
  • It does not store full message bodies, and holds no data on any machine other than the operator's own.

Access

Google account access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
